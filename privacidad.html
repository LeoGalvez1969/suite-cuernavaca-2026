II. What Data We Collect

To process your reservation and ensure a safe stay, we may collect:

Identification data

  • Full name
  • Nationality
  • Government-issued ID or passport (for check-in only)

Contact data

  • Email address
  • Phone / WhatsApp number
  • Country of residence

Reservation data

  • Check-in / check-out dates
  • Number of guests
  • Special requests

Financial data

  • Bank transfer reference (we never store card numbers)
  • Receipts and invoices
We do NOT collect sensitive data such as racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, political opinions, sexual orientation, or health information.

III. Purposes of Processing

Primary purposes (necessary for the service):

  • Reservation management: confirming bookings, scheduling check-in/check-out, communicating logistical details.
  • Identity verification: ID verification at check-in for security and legal compliance.
  • Payment processing: issuing receipts, tracking deposits, refunding the security deposit.
  • Compliance with the condominium administration: registering guest names with the front desk, in line with Las Palomas internal rules.
  • Direct support: WhatsApp concierge, emergency contact during the stay.

Secondary purposes (optional, may be objected to):

  • Sending future promotional offers about the property.
  • Requesting feedback or testimonials after the stay.

You may object to secondary purposes at any time by writing to lgalvez@nielsen-consulting.com with the subject line "Opt-out — Privacy Notice".

IV. Legal Basis

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual necessity (Art. 10 LFPDPPP / Art. 6.1.b GDPR): processing is required to fulfill the reservation contract.
  • Legal obligation: tax and accounting records, condominium guest registration.
  • Legitimate interest: fraud prevention, security, internal records.
  • Consent (for secondary purposes): you may withdraw consent at any time.

V. Data Sharing & Transfers

We do not sell, rent or commercialize your personal data. We may share limited information only with:

  • Las Palomas Condominium Administration: guest names and stay dates, as required by the internal regulations of the property.
  • Banking institutions: for processing transfers and refunds (your bank handles its own data).
  • Tax authorities (SAT): only when legally required for invoicing.
  • Hosting and email providers: Vercel (hosting of this site, USA) and Google/Gmail (email communications, USA). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security standards.
  • Authorities: when required by a valid legal order.

International transfers (e.g., to providers based in the USA) are protected under standard data protection clauses and applicable safeguards.

VI. Data Retention

  • Reservation and contact data: kept for up to 5 years after check-out, for legal, tax and warranty purposes.
  • ID copies: reviewed at check-in only, NOT stored permanently — deleted within 30 days after check-out.
  • Financial records: kept for 5 years (tax obligation).
  • Marketing data (if consented): kept until you object or withdraw consent.

VII. Security Measures

We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your data:

  • Encrypted storage (Google Drive / Gmail with two-factor authentication).
  • Access limited to the Host / Lessor only.
  • No transmission of card numbers — payments via direct bank transfer or trusted gateways.
  • HTTPS for the website (TLS encryption).
  • Regular review of stored documents and timely deletion when no longer needed.

VIII. Your ARCO Rights

Under Mexico's LFPDPPP (and equivalent rights under GDPR for EU/UK guests), you have the right to:

Your Rights · Derechos ARCO
  • Access — know what personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Cancellation — request deletion of your data when no longer needed (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Opposition — object to specific uses, including marketing.
  • Portability (GDPR) — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing.

To exercise any of these rights, send a request to lgalvez@nielsen-consulting.com with the subject line "ARCO Rights Request", including:

  • Your full name and a copy of an official ID (only for verification — not stored after the request is processed).
  • The specific right you are exercising and any details (e.g., what data should be corrected or deleted).
  • An email or phone where you wish to receive a response.

We will respond within 20 business days as required by Mexican law. If you reside in the EU/UK, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Mexico, you may file complaints with INAI (www.inai.org.mx).

IX. Cookies & Analytics

The Suite Cuernavaca website is a static page that does not use cookies, tracking pixels, advertising trackers, or analytics tools. The only third-party content embedded is:

  • Google Maps iframes, used to display routes from the property to tourist destinations and World Cup stadiums. These are loaded only when you click a destination card. Google may receive your IP address and basic browser data subject to its own privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp / mailto links, which redirect to your own messaging app or email client when clicked.

No data about your visit is sent back to us automatically.

X. Minors

The reservation must always be made by an adult (18+). Personal data of minors traveling as part of a family group is processed only as part of the head guest's reservation and is never used for marketing or shared with third parties beyond the strict purposes stated above.

XI. Changes to this Notice

We may update this notice to reflect legal, technical or operational changes. The current version is always available on this page, dated below. Material changes will be communicated by email to confirmed guests.

Las transferencias internacionales (por ejemplo, a proveedores ubicados en EE.UU.) están protegidas mediante cláusulas estándar de protección de datos y salvaguardas aplicables.
